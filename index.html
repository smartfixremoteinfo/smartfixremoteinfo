Who we are

Professional IT help, without the unnecessary hassle.

SmartFix is a Zimbabwean Information Technology company specialising in professional remote and managed IT support. Using secure remote-support technology, we help customers with software, operating systems, productivity tools, networking and cybersecurity. Where physical repairs are needed, qualified technicians can be scheduled for on-site assistance.

We also provide professional document and creative services, including CV design, document editing, graphics, business cards, typing and Excel data capture.

Our mission: Deliver fast, reliable, affordable and professional IT support that empowers customers through technology.

Our vision: Become Zimbabwe's leading provider of remote IT support and managed technology services.
