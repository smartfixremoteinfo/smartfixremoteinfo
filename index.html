FAQ

Common Questions

How does remote support work?

Message us on WhatsApp and explain the problem. If it can be fixed remotely, we arrange a session and connect to your computer over the internet, so you don't need to travel anywhere.

What do I need for remote support?

A working internet connection and a computer that can start up. If your computer won't turn on at all, tell us first so we can advise you.

What are your working hours?

Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. You can message us on WhatsApp at any time, and we'll reply during working hours.

How much does it cost?

Remote diagnosis is free and remote support is $8 per hour. Emergency support is $30. Single services like virus removal ($10), software installation ($10, productivity software from $15) or Windows installation ($20) have fixed prices, shown in the pricing section above.

How do I pay?

We accept EcoCash, InnBucks and bank transfer. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll send you the payment details once we've agreed on the service and price.

Can you help with CVs and documents?

Yes. We offer a Basic CV Revamp ($5), the Job Hunter Bundle ($10), an Executive / Diaspora Package ($15 to $20) and typing, formatting and editing from $1 per page. Send us your old CV on WhatsApp to get started.

How fast do you deliver CVs and documents?

We deliver within 24 hours. Your documents are kept confidential and handled professionally.

What is the difference between your CV packages?

The Basic CV Revamp ($5) gives you a modern, clean layout in PDF and Word with spelling and grammar fixes. The Job Hunter Bundle ($10) adds an ATS-friendly CV, a tailored cover letter and an editable Word file. The Executive / Diaspora Package ($15 to $20) is built to international standards (UK, SA or Canada format) and includes a cover letter and LinkedIn profile optimisation.

Do you offer a student discount?

Yes. Students get a special local discount. Message us on WhatsApp and ask about the student discount before you order.

Is remote support safe?

Yes. Nothing connects until you open our link and approve the request, you watch everything we do live, and you can stop the session at any moment. See exactly how we connect.

Will you see my personal files?

We only work on the problem you asked us to fix. You are watching your screen the whole time, and we recommend closing banking, email and personal files before we start.

Do I need to install anything?

In most cases you only open the link we send and accept the request. If something needs to be downloaded, we guide you step by step on WhatsApp.

How do I know a link is really from you?

We only send links from our official WhatsApp number, +263 773 092 198. If you are unsure about any message, do not click it. Message us first and we will confirm.

How long does a session take?

It depends on the problem. Simple jobs like installing software can be quick, while things like a Windows installation take longer. We will tell you what to expect before we start.

What if my problem cannot be fixed remotely?

We will tell you honestly. Hardware faults such as a broken screen or a failing hard drive need a physical repair, and we will advise you on the best next step.

Which computers and systems do you support?

We work on Windows, Linux and macOS computers. If you have a phone, tablet or another device, message us and we will tell you whether we can help.

Will I lose my files if you reinstall Windows?

Before any installation we ask what you need to keep, so we can plan around it. Where possible, back up your important files first.

Do you offer monthly support for businesses?

Yes. Our Growth Plan is $100 per month for small teams, the Pay-As-You-Go plan is $55 per month for individuals, and larger organisations can request a custom quote. See the IT Plans section for the details.

How much do graphics designs cost?

Flyers, posters, business cards and social media graphics are $10 each. Logos and banners are $15 each. Message us on WhatsApp to get started.

How much does a website cost?

Websites start from $80 for a landing page, $100 for a personal portfolio, $150 for a business, company or church site, $200 for a school or restaurant site and $250 for an online store. Each package includes responsive design, a contact form and a WhatsApp button, with page counts and extras listed on each website sample. The final price depends on your pages and features, so we confirm a quote after you tell us what you want.

What do I send you for a CV?

Send your contact details, work history, education, skills and the type of job you are applying for. Just send your old CV and message us “READY” on WhatsApp. We will revamp it as a modern or ATS-friendly CV and deliver within 24 hours, in the file format you need, such as Word or PDF.

Can you help customers outside Chegutu?

Yes. Remote support and online design work only need a good internet connection, so you can be anywhere. We will agree on payment together before we start.

Not sure what you need?

Tell us what is going wrong in your own words and we will point you to the right fix. No technical terms needed.
